What we install

Three ways to light a car

All of it is fiber and LED behind the trim — nothing taped to a surface, nothing you can see in daylight.

Starlight headliner

The headliner comes out of the car. Fiber strands get pulled through the fabric one at a time, trimmed flush, and fed back to an RGBW engine tucked behind a panel.

Points
200 to 600+, your call on density
Shooting stars
Optional, added as their own channel
Control
App or remote — color, brightness, twinkle
Fits
Factory headliners, sunroof cars, rear rows

Ambient interior

Door cards, footwells, dash trim, console and cupholders. Strips sit in diffuser channel, so you get a clean line of light instead of a row of dots.

Process

How an install runs

  1. 01

    Send the car and the idea

    Year, make, model, and roughly how busy you want the ceiling. A photo of the interior helps. You get a price back, not a range.

  2. 02

    Book the slot

    Deposit holds the day. Drop the car in Peoria in the morning — most starlight jobs are a full day, bigger builds run two.

  3. 03

    Panels come down

    Headliner out, trim off, fiber pulled and routed, engine mounted somewhere dry and reachable. Everything goes back on factory clips.

  4. 04

    Tune it with you in the car

    We set your scenes together — daily color, show color, brightness at night — then you drive it out already dialed in.

FAQ

Before you book

Does pulling the headliner wreck it?

No — it's designed to come out. The board unclips, the fiber gets pulled through from the back, and the strands sit flush with the fabric. Once it's back in, you can't tell it was out until the lights are on.

Is underglow actually legal in Arizona?

Partly. Arizona restricts lights that show red or blue toward the front of the vehicle, and anything flashing or rotating — that's reserved for emergency vehicles. So the build gets set up with a street scene that stays inside that, and your reds and blues live on a separate scene you run at shows and meets. Worth confirming for your own setup before you drive it.

Will it drain my battery?

Not if it's wired right. Power gets tapped to a switched source, so the whole system dies with the ignition instead of sitting live overnight off an always-on circuit.

How long is the car with you?

Most starlight headliners are a single day. Add a second row, ambient zones or underglow and it's usually two. You'll know which before you book.

Can you match a color I already have?

Yes. The engines are RGBW, so we can land on your factory ambient color, your wrap, or whatever you're chasing — and save it as the default scene.

What if a strand goes out later?

Bring it back. Fiber doesn't burn out the way an LED does, so a dead point is almost always the engine or a connection, and both are quick to sort.
